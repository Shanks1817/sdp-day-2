Our Green Vision

Our college aims to create an environmentally responsible campus where students, faculty and staff work together to conserve natural resources and reduce environmental impact.


Through renewable energy, water conservation, tree plantation, sustainable transportation and responsible waste management, the campus encourages students to turn environmental awareness into everyday action.

Solar Power on Campus

Solar panels can be installed on rooftops, parking areas and other suitable spaces to generate clean electricity for campus facilities.


Solar energy can help reduce electricity consumption from conventional sources and also provides students with a real-world example of renewable energy technology.

Waste Management

Segregate, reduce, reuse and recycle.

🟢 Wet Waste: Food scraps and biodegradable waste can be composted.
🔵 Dry Waste: Paper, cardboard, glass and suitable plastics should be segregated for recycling.
🔴 E-Waste: Electronic waste should be collected separately and sent to authorized recyclers.
🟡 Reduce Plastic: Encourage reusable bottles, bags and containers instead of single-use plastics.
